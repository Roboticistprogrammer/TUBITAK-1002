# Cross-architecture comparison

MobileNetV3-Large, ResNet-50 and the three SwinV2-B students (KD routed, KD uniform, no KD), all seed 42, on the FASDD test split (20,435 images) and on the Jetson Orin. Generated by `scripts/plot_cross_architecture.py` from the per-architecture `predictions/` and `benchmarks/` folders; accuracy is the TensorRT FP16 engine, "macro accuracy" is the equal-weight mean of the CV, UAV and RS accuracies. Latency, energy and throughput are from random 192x192 input, preprocessing excluded (see the top-level README for the protocol and caveats).

## What the data supports

- **Accuracy:** KD routed has the highest macro accuracy (91.99%), then KD uniform (91.30), no KD (91.19), MobileNetV3-Large (91.06) and ResNet-50 (90.12). In `fig2` the 95% intervals overlap for every pair except ResNet-50 against KD routed and KD uniform on CV; the RS panel (n = 370) has very wide intervals. Overlapping intervals are a conservative unpaired check; the paired exact McNemar tests in each `<arch>/figures/metrics.md` are more sensitive. They show: ResNet-50 is worse than all three students on CV (p <= 0.002); MobileNetV3-Large vs KD routed is split (KD routed better on CV, p = 0.005; MobileNetV3-Large better on UAV, p < 0.001); MobileNetV3-Large vs KD uniform shows no significant difference in any domain; MobileNetV3-Large vs no KD differs only on UAV (MobileNetV3-Large better, p < 0.001).
- **Cost:** MobileNetV3-Large is 2.6x faster than the SwinV2-B students at batch 1 (4.6 vs 11.9 ms), uses about 6x less energy per image (27 vs 165 mJ) and its engine is 19x smaller (9.3 vs 173.7 MiB). It reaches 1144 img/s at batch 8 with TensorRT.
- **ResNet-50** is less accurate than MobileNetV3-Large and also slower, more energy-hungry and larger in every measured setting, so it is dominated in `fig1`.
- **Runtimes (batch 1):** TensorRT FP16 is 1.2x (MobileNetV3-Large), 1.6x (ResNet-50) and about 3x (SwinV2-B) faster than ORT CUDA; ORT CPU is 4-11x slower than ORT CUDA.

Single seed only; seed-to-seed variation is not yet measured. Seeds 43 and 44 are training; regenerate with `python scripts/plot_cross_architecture.py` once they and their engines exist.
